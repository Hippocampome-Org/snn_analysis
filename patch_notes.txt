Patch #1
getspikephase.m:
Changed
peaks=[peaks(1:end-1)'; peaks(end):mean(diff(peaks)):(max(activitytimes)+mean(diff(peaks)))];
To
peaks=[peaks(1:end-1) peaks(end):mean(diff(peaks)):(max(activitytimes)+mean(diff(peaks)))];

Issue:
This avoids "Error using vertcat Dimensions of arrays being concatenated are not consistent."

     MRVLResultFilt = [MRVLResultFilt; i*ones(size(MRVLFilt,1),1), ...
                       MRVLFilt];

to

and not to work with it.
